## What to Check Before Getting Started

Confirm your PC against [Check system compatibility](/destiny-2-cheat-system-requirements/) first. Do not start installs if HVCI, TPM, Secure Boot, or Hyper-V requirements fail.

Also confirm the live Destiny 2 build status on the merchant listing. Patch days can change supported clients.

## License Activation and Access

After payment, the merchant emails access details for the plan you bought. Download and activate only from that receipt or portal—never from third-party mirrors.

Keep your order email handy when you open a support ticket. Product overview: [Destiny 2 cheats product details](/).

## Understanding the Required Cloud DMA Setup

Full functionality on supported builds uses Cloud DMA: a Hyper-V virtual environment on the same PC, plus separately purchased GREENWARD, Static Server, and AWS services.

Read [Cloud DMA setup information](/cloud-dma-for-destiny-2/) before enabling Hyper-V. Cloud DMA is not included in the main Destiny 2 license price.

## Configuration and Support Information

## Frequently Asked Setup Questions

### How do I install Destiny 2 cheats?

Pay on the live listing, open the merchant portal from your receipt, then follow the current installer notes for your Windows edition and Cloud DMA path.

### What if Hyper-V will not enable?

Stop and fix firmware virtualization / Windows edition issues first. Cloud DMA cannot proceed without Hyper-V availability.

### Where do I get help after purchase?

Use merchant support with your order email. This site explains listed steps; it does not issue keys.

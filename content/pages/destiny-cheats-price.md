## What the Destiny 2 cheat cost includes

Listed features cover adjustable aimbot settings, player ESP, 2D radar, recoil/spread helpers, full bright, custom crosshair, and stream-proof overlay.

## Cloud DMA is a separate purchase

Cloud DMA is not bundled into the $35 or $150 Destiny 2 license. Its listed lifetime price is $50.

That layer also requires separately purchased:

- GREENWARD.
- Static Server.
- AWS.

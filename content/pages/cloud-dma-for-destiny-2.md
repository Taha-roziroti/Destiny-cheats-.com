## How the Single-PC Hyper-V Setup Works

The product provisions a virtual environment through Hyper-V on the same gaming PC and uses an encrypted service link for activation. The listing does not require a second PC, USB DMA bridge, or PCIe DMA card.

Follow installer notes for your Windows edition. Availability of Hyper-V varies—especially on Home builds.

# Can Destiny 2 Cheats Get You Banned?

Yes—using third-party cheats can lead to account or device restrictions under Bungie’s published Destiny 2 rules. This page summarizes how to read those policies before you buy anything.

## Does Bungie Allow Third-Party Cheats?

No. Bungie’s published materials treat unauthorized third-party tools that modify or unfairly advantage gameplay as prohibited. This affiliate site explains a merchant listing; it does not authorize rule-breaking.

## What Bungie's Published Policy Says

Bungie publishes player conduct and security policies that cover cheating, exploits, and unauthorized software. Always read the current official policy pages on Bungie’s site—wording can change.

## Account Bans vs. Device Bans

Enforcement can affect accounts, linked platforms, or hardware identifiers depending on the case and Bungie’s current tooling. Merchants may sell separate HWID-related utilities; those do not remove platform risk.

If you cannot afford to lose access, do not run third-party cheats.
